Ready to count?

Position your camera to your side, around floor height. Keep your head, hands, hips, knees and ankles visible.

Knees: not tracking · Pose: — FPS

Set your floor line

Press Start, get into a plank (side-on, or diagonal in 3D view), and the app finds your hands and feet, checks your knees are off the floor, then locks a cyan ground line after a 3-2-1 countdown. The yellow slider line is only a fallback before the lock.

The line is an image-based reference, not a physical floor sensor. It only works well when the floor appears approximately level in the image.

Tracking & counting

Turn on your camera to begin.

Go to a straight-arm plank to arm the counter; lower until your elbow bends and push back up.

Reps that do not reach the depth target still count, but are flagged as shallow.

A rep requires a stable top, meaningful descent and return to your calibrated lockout. Counting pauses if hand support or camera framing appears lost; recalibrate to resume. A rep is flagged (not discarded) for knees near the floor, sagging/piked hips, or shallow depth.

Privacy: Video and pose estimation stay in your browser. This app downloads a pretrained MediaPipe model and its runtime on first use; no paid API or video upload. Results are approximate and not a validated exercise or clinical assessment.
