Ready to count?

Position your camera to your side, around floor height. Keep your head, hands, hips, knees and ankles visible.

Knees: not tracking · Pose: — FPS

Set your floor line

Move the slider so the dashed yellow line overlays the floor near your hands, knees and feet. Put the camera at floor height, side-on, and keep your full body inside the frame.

The line is an image-based reference, not a physical floor sensor. It only works well when the floor appears approximately level in the image.

Tracking & counting

Turn on your camera to begin.

Go to a straight-arm plank to arm the counter; lower until your elbow bends and push back up.

A rep counts when your elbow goes below 100° and returns above 155°. Knees near the calibrated floor are flagged, not automatically treated as proof of contact.

Privacy: Video and pose estimation stay in your browser. This app downloads a pretrained MediaPipe model and its runtime on first use; no paid API or video upload. Results are approximate and not a validated exercise or clinical assessment.
